# Intellectual property and licensing — specification tree

This specification tree (`spec/`) is licensed Apache License 2.0 (the
LICENSE copy beside this document; the same terms as the reference
implementation).

- **Patent posture.** Contributors license essential claims covering
  their contributions under Apache-2.0's patent grant (Section 3). The
  steward's intent is a donation-ready posture: no CLA is required,
  and no additional patent covenant is asserted beyond Apache-2.0. A
  future standards venue's IPR process (W3C-mode essential-claims
  disclosure, or a foundation's inbound license) can be satisfied from
  this basis without re-licensing.
- **Sign-off.** Contributions are accepted under the Developer
  Certificate of Origin (DCO): `git commit -s`. The sign-off records
  that the contributor wrote or has the right to submit the work.
- **Copyright.** Copyright is retained by contributors, licensed under
  Apache-2.0; the NOTICE file beside this tree names the steward.
- **Trademark.** The project name carries no registered trademark
  claim by the steward at this time; a receiving foundation may adopt
  one as part of a transfer.
